You already know how it ends.

Now tell me how it begins.

there are NO rules

be revelation before genesis

be love after death

feminine and masculine united

be reborn - an infant, child, elderly and go backwards until you’re intention.

have pride, greed, lust, envy, gluttony, wrath, sloth - aren’t those the seven heavenly virtues of salvation?

live in the past of the year 2369.

live in a future where time doesn’t exist.

you’re a dragon, a fairy, a giant wolf, invisible elf, golden tree, you’re Jesus sharing breakfast with Judas.

be moons of Saturn and the Karma itself combined in all non-existing beings, locking all existing beings forever on Earth.

be fire. air. earth. water. ether. be comets and galaxies. be so many stars until you discover yourself a black hole.

finally, let your imagination fall into silence, and find yourself in the beginning of ONE.

there are NO rules.
